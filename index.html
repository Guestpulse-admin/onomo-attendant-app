Wipe tables/chairs. Clean table bases. Remove floor marks. Empty bins. Ensure neat presentation.

Vacuum carpets. Spot-clean walls/skirting. Wipe lift doors and buttons. Clear room service trays if visible.

Empty bins. Wipe canteen tables and counters. Sweep/mop canteen floor. Ensure staff areas are presentable.

Toilets (Lobby, Rest, Staff)

Clean & sanitise urinals/toilets. Floors dry. Replenish toilet paper, soap, towels. Empty bins. No odours.
